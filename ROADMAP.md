# Roadmap

Planned upgrades for Hana. Nothing here is built yet. Each item lists the conditions it must meet before it is added, and how to add it.

## Rules for every upgrade

1. **Free and open source only.** Every part, including any app a business is asked to install, must be under an open-source licence. No paid service and no API key.
2. **Tested and proven first.** An upgrade is added only after it has been tested on real phones and has a track record. New or untested tools wait here.
3. **Backwards compatible.** Older menus, orders, saved data and links must keep working:
   - new fields in `menu.json`, `list.json`, orders and messages are optional; readers ignore fields they do not know;
   - message formats keep their version tag (`hana1`, `HANA1`, `hana-update-1`, `hana_vault: 1`); a change that breaks old readers gets a new tag, and the old tag stays readable;
   - saved data on phones is upgraded in place on first load, never discarded;
   - `tools/check.py` and `tools/browser_test.py` must pass before and after.
4. **Light and fast.** Nothing new loads on the home page; a feature loads only on the screen that uses it, with a size limit in `check.py`.

## Planned

### One order list for every app (business tools)

- **What:** new-order notifications from the other apps a business uses (WhatsApp, text messages, other delivery apps) appear in Hana's order list, with a sound, so staff watch one screen. Accepting still happens in each app.
- **How:** an open-source Android notification forwarder on the shop phone posts each notification to the business's private ntfy topic; the business tools list it.
- **Waiting for:** a forwarder that is open source, on F-Droid, maintained for at least a year and tested with ntfy and with the delivery apps used in Chittagong. The current candidate, Message487 (MIT), was first published in October 2026 and is untested with ntfy.

### Recording sales from other apps, and profit per platform

- **What:** each sale records its source (Hana, another delivery app, phone, walk-in) and that platform's commission, so statements show what each platform really leaves the business.
- **How:** an optional `source` and `commission_percent` on point-of-sale records; old records count as "not stated".
- **Waiting for:** testing with two or more real businesses.

### Open menu feed

- **What:** each listing's menu published as plain JSON and CSV at a fixed address, for any open-source tool or platform that reads such files.
- **How:** generated by `tools/build.py`, next to `feed.xml`.
- **Waiting for:** an agreed column layout, tested with at least one consuming tool.

### Claiming and publishing from a phone, with no forge account

- **What:** a business claims its listing and publishes menu changes from the business tools alone, with no GitHub or Codeberg account.
- **How:** the business tools already sign every menu with the owner's key. The signed menu is posted to a community ntfy topic; a scheduled GitHub Action in the Hana repository collects it, checks the signature against the owner key named in the claim, runs `tools/check.py` and commits it. The signature proves only that the owner's key signed the menu, not who the business is, so the first claim is still vouched for by two community members, who confirm the business by phone or in person and link its key to the listing. After that, no person needs write access on the owner's behalf.
- **Waiting for:** a design review of how the owner's key is first linked to the verified business, how duplicate or replayed submissions are rejected (menu versions and hash chains already order them), and how the scheduled workflow handles untrusted submissions without exposing its write credentials; abuse limits on the topic; and a test with two or more real businesses. Existing forge-based claims keep working unchanged.

### Amended orders, agreed by both sides

- **What:** when a business cannot fill an order exactly (an item sold out, a different price), it proposes a change in the app, and the customer accepts or declines it. The order record keeps the original, the proposal and the answer as separate signed events.
- **How:** a new signed update type (`amend`) on orders sent in Hana, using the existing signing and sealing; older apps show it as an ordinary update and the customer can still reply in chat.
- **Waiting for:** testing with real businesses to learn how often amendments happen and what they look like, so the screen stays simple.

### Named baskets and recent searches

- **What:** a regular customer saves a basket as "usual breakfast" or "office lunch", and the search box remembers recent searches, both on the phone only with an easy clear button.
- **Waiting for:** evidence from usability sessions that "Order again" and favourites are not already enough.

### Others under consideration

- Splitting `list.json` by zone once it nears its size limit.
- Wider Bangla coverage (sentences and notices), with every page checked at 320 px.
- A larger Bangla search word list, including romanised spellings such as "biriyani" and "murgi", built from searches that real customers report finding nothing for.
- Automatic zone matching by drawn area boundaries, once the boundaries exist in `areas.json`.
