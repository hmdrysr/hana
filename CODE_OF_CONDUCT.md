# Code of conduct

Hana serves ordinary people and small businesses. Treat everyone who takes part with respect.

## Expected

- Be polite and stay on the topic.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you accuse.
- Say what you checked and what you did not.
- Protect other people's privacy.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, slurs or abuse of any kind.
- Posting anyone's private information, including customers, couriers, restaurant staff and other contributors. This includes names, phone numbers, addresses, NID numbers, photos and order details.
- Spam, advertising or paid placement requests.
- Fake listings, fake pledges, fake vouches or fake reviews.
- Pressure on a maintainer or voucher to approve something.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn a person, or block them from the repo. Every action is recorded with a reason. A person who is blocked can appeal under `GOVERNANCE.md` section 5.

Report a problem with a person's conduct to the contacts in `VOLUNTEERS.md`. If someone is in danger, contact the police first.

This file is short on purpose. The project may adopt a longer published code of conduct later (UNVERIFIED: check its licence before copying one).
